More Than A Resume

We want to understand what matters to you.

We want to understand what you're good at, what you're looking for and what matters to you in your next role. That helps us introduce you to opportunities that actually make sense for your experience and goals.

How We Work With Candidates

A clear, respectful process.

You stay in control of your information and your next move. We only move forward when an opportunity makes sense and you are comfortable being represented.

01

Understand

We learn about your experience, strengths, career goals, compensation expectations and what would make a move worthwhile.

02

Match

When a relevant opportunity comes up, we share the role context first so you can decide whether it is worth exploring.

03

Represent

If you want to move forward, we introduce you with care and support communication throughout the hiring process.

Your information stays confidential.

We will not send your resume or personal information to an employer without discussing the opportunity with you first and receiving your permission to represent you.

There is never a fee for candidates.

Meriquo's recruitment fees are paid by employers. Candidates are never charged for being represented or placed by Meriquo.

Not actively looking? That's okay.

Some of the best career conversations happen before someone starts searching. If you're open to hearing about the right opportunity, we'd still be happy to connect.
