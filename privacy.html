Privacy Policy

Meriquo keeps personal information limited, relevant and handled with care throughout recruitment conversations.

Last updated August 21, 2026

Questions or removal requests can be sent directly to moe@meriquo.com.

This policy explains how Meriquo handles information shared by clients, candidates and website visitors. The goal is simple: collect only what is needed, use it for relevant recruitment communication, and respect confidentiality.

01

Information We Collect

Meriquo may collect details you choose to provide through email, phone, LinkedIn, direct contact or the contact form. This can include your name, company, role, contact details, hiring needs, resume details or career preferences.

02

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, discuss searches, assess potential candidate fit, coordinate introductions and provide recruitment-related follow-up.

03

Candidate Confidentiality

Candidate conversations are handled with discretion. Meriquo does not present a candidate to a client without relevant discussion and alignment.

04

No Sale of Personal Information

Meriquo does not sell personal information. Candidate, client and inquiry details are used only for relevant communication, recruitment discussion and search-related activity.
