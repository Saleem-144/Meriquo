Meriquo was founded by Moazam (Moe) Aslam, a recruiting leader with more than 20 years of experience helping organizations identify, attract, and hire exceptional talent across North America.

Moe's background includes work with major global staffing and recruitment firms including Randstad, Adecco, and ManpowerGroup. That experience shaped a search practice built on disciplined process, market understanding, and clear communication.

Today, Meriquo focuses on executive recruiting, technical recruitment, professional hiring, and contingency search for clients that want a thoughtful boutique partner.

The firm is intentionally personal. Clients are not passed between layers of account management. They work with Moe directly, from the first conversation through the final hiring decision.

When you work with Meriquo, you work directly with me.
