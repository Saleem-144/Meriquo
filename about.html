Meriquo was founded by Moe Aslam, who brings more than 15 years of recruitment experience across North America, including senior roles with Randstad, ManpowerGroup and Adecco.

After more than 15 years in recruitment, Moe founded Meriquo with a simple idea: keep recruiting focused on what actually matters. Understand what a company needs, find people who genuinely fit, and don't overwhelm clients with resumes just to show activity.

Today, Meriquo focuses primarily on Technology & IT Recruitment, helping companies find talent across software, AI, data, cybersecurity, cloud, infrastructure and technology leadership. We also provide Executive Search for senior and critical leadership positions.

Meriquo combines the experience and search capabilities of a larger recruitment firm with a more focused, personal approach.

Good recruiting is not about sending the most resumes. It is about introducing people who are genuinely worth meeting.
