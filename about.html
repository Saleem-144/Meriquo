Focused recruitment across North America.

Recruiting kept focused on what actually matters.

Meriquo was founded by Moe Aslam, who brings more than 15 years of recruitment experience across North America, including senior roles with Randstad, ManpowerGroup and Adecco.

After more than 15 years in recruitment, Moe founded Meriquo with a simple idea: understand what a company needs, find people who genuinely fit, and avoid overwhelming clients with resumes just to show activity.

Today, Meriquo focuses primarily on Technology & IT Recruitment, helping companies find talent across software, AI, data, cybersecurity, cloud, infrastructure and technology leadership. Meriquo also provides Executive Search for senior and critical leadership positions.
