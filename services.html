How It Works

Designed for employers who want better hiring conversations.

Meriquo begins with a practical intake conversation to clarify what the role really requires, why it is open, what has worked before, and where the candidate market may be tight.

From there, the search is managed with focus: targeted outreach, thoughtful screening, concise feedback, and a shortlist built around merit rather than volume.
